SCANNER LEASE AGREEMENT

{{ p.client_practice_name }}

This Scanner Lease Agreement (“Agreement”) is made effective as of  , 20__, by and between {{ lab.name }}{% if lab.address %}, of {{ lab.address }}{% endif %} (“Lab”), and {{ p.client_practice_name }}{% if p.client_address %}, a dental practice with offices at {{ p.client_address }}{% endif %} (“Customer”).

1. EQUIPMENT

Lab agrees to lease to Customer one (1) intraoral scanner, one (1) computer/laptop, and related configuration/set-up and online training (collectively, the “Equipment”).

2. PRICE AND DISCOUNT

The standard price for the Equipment is {{ (p.results.doctor.discounted_price + p.inputs.get('discount', 0))|usd }}. In consideration of Customer's commitment to this Agreement and a down payment of {{ (p.inputs.get('doctor_down_payment', 0))|usd }}, Lab will reduce the price by {{ (p.inputs.get('discount', 0))|usd }}, for a total of {{ (p.results.doctor.discounted_price)|usd }} (the “Adjusted Price”), which is the basis for the Lease Payment calculated below.

3. LEASE PAYMENTS

Customer shall pay Lab a down payment of {{ (p.inputs.get('doctor_down_payment', 0))|usd }} upon execution of this Agreement. The remaining {{ (p.results.doctor.financed)|usd }} is amortized over {{ p.inputs.get('doctor_term', 0)|int }} months at Lab's current lease rate, resulting in a monthly payment of {{ (p.results.doctor.monthly_payment)|usd }} (the “Lease Payment”), due on the same day of each month as the down payment, beginning thirty (30) days after delivery and installation of the Equipment, and subject to the monthly credit in Section 4 below.

4. MONTHLY DOCTOR INVOICE CREDIT

Each month, Lab will apply {{ (p.inputs.get('credit_pct', 0))|pct1 }} of Customer's total monthly invoice toward that month's Lease Payment, as follows. Customer is not required to send any minimum amount or exclusive share of its business to Lab — this credit simply applies to whatever Lab bills Customer that month:

(a) Invoices of {{ (p.inputs.get('low_threshold', 0))|usd }} or less: no credit applies; the full {{ (p.results.doctor.monthly_payment)|usd }} Lease Payment is due.
(b) Invoices greater than {{ (p.inputs.get('low_threshold', 0))|usd }} {% if p.results.credit_program.breakeven_spend %}and up to {{ (p.results.credit_program.breakeven_spend)|usd }}{% endif %}: Customer owes the Lease Payment less {{ (p.inputs.get('credit_pct', 0))|pct1 }} of that month's invoice.
{% if p.results.credit_program.breakeven_spend %} (c) Invoices greater than {{ (p.results.credit_program.breakeven_spend)|usd }}: no Lease Payment is due for that month. {% endif %}

Any amount owed after the credit is applied shall be paid together with that month's invoice. Any credit offered toward the scanner lease payment requires on time payment of the monthly invoice.

5. OWNERSHIP

The Equipment is leased, not sold. Lab retains sole ownership and title to the Equipment at all times during this Agreement. Customer shall not sell, encumber, sublease, or permit a lien against the Equipment, and bears the risk of loss for the Equipment at Customer's practice location, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

6. EQUIPMENT LOCATION

Customer shall keep the Equipment at the practice location identified above throughout the term of this Agreement and shall not relocate the Equipment without Lab's prior written consent.

7. CANCELLATION

Customer may cancel this Agreement at any time upon sixty (60) days' written notice to Lab. Upon cancellation, Customer shall (a) return the Equipment to Lab at Customer's own expense within that notice period, (b) pay the two (2) Lease Payments due for that final sixty (60) day notice period ({{ (p.results.doctor.monthly_payment * 2)|usd }}), and (c) pay an early termination fee equal to one (1) full Lease Payment ({{ (p.results.doctor.monthly_payment)|usd }}). Cancellation does not relieve Customer of any other Lease Payments or invoice balances already due through the notice period.

8. END OF LEASE TERM

At the end of the Lease term, Lab will determine, in its sole discretion, how to proceed with Customer. Lab's options include, without limitation: (a) offering Customer an upgrade to then-current equipment at the price Lab quotes at that time; or (b) continuing the lease on a month-to-month basis at the then-current Lease Payment. Lab will notify Customer of its determination before the end of the Lease term.

9. DELIVERY AND TRAINING

Lab will configure/set-up, deliver and provide online training on the Equipment in conjunction with the distributor at Customer's practice location within a mutually agreed timeframe following execution of this Agreement.

10. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

Each of the following is a material default: (a) failure to make any Lease Payment when due — a single missed payment is sufficient; (b) relocating the Equipment without Lab's consent; or (c) Customer's insolvency or bankruptcy. Lab shall give Customer written notice of any default and five (5) days to cure it. If the default is not cured, Lab may terminate this Agreement and repossess the Equipment, in addition to any other remedy available at law.

11. WARRANTIES; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Lab warrants the Equipment will be free of substantive defects in material and workmanship for one (1) year from delivery. LAB SHALL NOT BE LIABLE FOR ANY INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES OF ANY NATURE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION; GOVERNING LAW

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of {{ lab.state or '[Lab State]' }}, without regard to its conflicts of law principles.

13. MISCELLANEOUS

This Agreement, including any attached quotation, is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements on that subject. It may be amended only by a writing signed by both parties. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect. A party's failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of its right to do so later.
