EQUIPMENT LEASE AGREEMENT

This Equipment Lease Agreement (“Agreement”) is made effective as of  , 20__, by and between {{ supplier.name }}{% if supplier.address %}, of {{ supplier.address }}{% endif %} (“Supplier”), and {{ lab.owner_name or '[Owner Name]' }}, individually, as the owner of {{ lab.name }}{% if lab.address %}, located at {{ lab.address }}{% endif %} (collectively with {{ lab.name }}, “Leasee”). All payment and other obligations under this agreement shall be the collective responsibility of the Leasee. Leasee intends to make the Equipment available to its own customer, {{ p.client_practice_name }}, under a separate agreement between Leasee and that customer; this Agreement governs only the relationship between Supplier and Leasee.

1. EQUIPMENT

Supplier agrees to lease to Leasee one (1) intraoral scanner, one (1) computer/laptop, and related set-up and online training (collectively, the “Equipment”), for Leasee's use in its own business, including placing the Equipment at the office of Leasee's customer.

2. PRICE

The price of the Equipment to Leasee is {{ (p.results.lab.equipment_cost)|usd }}. Leasee shall pay a down payment of {{ (p.results.lab.down_payment)|usd }} upon execution of this Agreement, with the remaining {{ (p.results.lab.financed)|usd }} amortized as set out in Section 3 below.

3. LEASE PAYMENTS

The amount financed is amortized over {{ p.inputs.get('lab_term', 0)|int }} months at Supplier's current lease rate, resulting in a monthly payment of {{ (p.results.lab.monthly_payment)|usd }} (the “Lease Payment”), due on the same day of each month as the down payment, beginning thirty (30) days after delivery of the Equipment.

4. OWNERSHIP

The Equipment is leased, not sold. Supplier retains sole ownership and title to the Equipment at all times during the Lease term. Leasee shall not sell, encumber, or permit a lien against the Equipment, and bears the risk of loss for the Equipment (whether at Leasee's own location or at a customer's location) at all times, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

5. EQUIPMENT LOCATION

Leasee may keep the Equipment at its own facility or place it at the office of a Leasee customer in the ordinary course of Leasee's business of providing the Equipment to its own customers, provided Leasee remains fully responsible for the Equipment and for informing Supplier, upon request, of its current location.

6. CANCELLATION

Leasee may cancel this Agreement at any time upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Supplier. Upon cancellation, Leasee shall (a) return the Equipment to Supplier at Leasee's own expense within that notice period, (b) pay the Lease Payment due for that final thirty (30) day notice period ({{ (p.results.lab.monthly_payment)|usd }}), and (c) pay an early termination fee equal to one-half (1/2) of one Lease Payment ({{ (p.results.lab.monthly_payment * 0.5)|usd }}). Cancellation does not relieve Leasee of any other Lease Payments already due through the notice period.

7. END OF LEASE TERM AND UPGRADE OPTION

Upon completion of {{ p.inputs.get('lab_term', 0)|int }} monthly Lease Payments, Leasee may take ownership of the Equipment, free of Supplier's security interest, by making one (1) final payment equal to a Lease Payment ({{ (p.results.lab.monthly_payment)|usd }}) (the “{{ p.inputs.get('lab_term', 0)|int + 1 }}th-month payment”).

Alternatively, at any time between month {{ p.inputs.get('lab_term', 0)|int }} and month {{ p.inputs.get('doctor_term', 0)|int }} of this Agreement, Supplier will, upon request, quote Leasee an upgrade to then-current equipment at the price in effect at that time. If Leasee accepts the upgrade, Leasee may swap the Equipment currently placed with its customer for the upgraded equipment, and Leasee shall have the remaining time before month {{ p.inputs.get('doctor_term', 0)|int }} of this Agreement to reach agreement with that customer on updated terms reflecting the upgraded equipment.

8. DELIVERY AND TRAINING

Supplier will configure/set-up, deliver and provide online training on the Equipment within a mutually agreed timeframe following execution of this Agreement, either to Leasee directly or, at Leasee's direction, to Leasee's customer location.

9. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

Each of the following is a material default: (a) failure to make any Lease Payment when due — a single missed payment is sufficient; (b) Leasee's insolvency or bankruptcy. Supplier shall give Leasee written notice of any default and five (5) days to cure it. If the default is not cured, Supplier may terminate this Agreement and repossess the Equipment, in addition to any other remedy available at law.

10. WARRANTIES; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Supplier warrants the Equipment will be free of substantive defects in material and workmanship for one (1) year from delivery. SUPPLIER SHALL NOT BE LIABLE FOR ANY INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES OF ANY NATURE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION; GOVERNING LAW

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflicts of law principles.

12. MISCELLANEOUS

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements on that subject. It may be amended only by a writing signed by both parties. If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect. A party's failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver of its right to do so later.
